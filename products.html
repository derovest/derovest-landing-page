Why we build

Why a services company
owns products.

Building and running our own products means we carry the same decisions our clients do — what to build, what to leave out, and what breaks at two in the morning.

We make the trade-offs

Not just recommend them. When we say something is not worth building yet, it is because we have made that call on our own roadmap.

Product work feeds client work

What we learn running Mentivax and PORTVAX goes straight into the software we build for other people.

Close to real users

Schools and traders, with real complaints and real edge cases — not a requirements document written by somebody who will never use it.
