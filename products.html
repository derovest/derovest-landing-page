WHY WE BUILD

Why a services company owns products

Building and running our own products means we carry the same decisions our clients do — what to build, what to leave out, what breaks at two in the morning. That tends to make for better advice than a supplier who only ever bills for the building.

  • We make the trade-offs ourselves, not just recommend them
  • What we learn running products feeds the software we build for others
  • Product work keeps us close to real users, not just requirements documents
