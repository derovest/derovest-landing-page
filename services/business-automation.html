Business Automation.

If your team repeatedly does the same manual task, we can look at whether technology can do it instead.

Talk to Derovest All services
§01
What it is

What this actually is.

You do not need to become a technology company to benefit from technology. Most businesses have a handful of processes that quietly consume hours every week — copying data, rebuilding the same report, chasing the same approvals.

WHO IT IS FOR

businesses that want to reduce repetitive manual work

Typical problems we get called about:

  • Someone capable spends a day a week on copying and retyping
  • The same report is rebuilt by hand every week or month
  • Two systems hold the same data and disagree with each other
  • A process stops entirely when one particular person is on leave
WHAT IT COVERS

What's included

  • Repetitive data entry between systems
  • Manual reporting and spreadsheet-based workflows
  • Repetitive administrative and back-office tasks
  • Approval and hand-off processes that rely on someone remembering
  • Connecting business tools that do not talk to each other
  • Custom workflow automation built around your process
§02
How it works

How the engagement works.

01

Look at the process

We follow the work as it actually happens — who does what, in which system, and where it stalls.

02

Work out what it costs

Hours against your own cost per hour. Plain arithmetic on numbers you already have, not a projection.

03

Decide what is worth automating

Not everything should be automated. We tell you which parts are worth it and which are not.

04

Build it

One process at a time, running alongside the manual version until your team trusts it.

05

Measure it

The same baseline, measured again after, so you can see what actually changed.

Have something that needs doing?

Describe it in a line or two. You'll get a straight answer about whether we can help and what it would take.
