Technology Staffing.

Technology talent through contract and hiring models, screened by people who can read code.

Talk to Derovest All services
§01
What it is

What this actually is.

Hiring technology people is slow and hard to judge from a CV. We help two ways: professionals on contract when you need capacity now, and technical screening support when you are hiring permanently.

WHO IT IS FOR

companies that need technology professionals

Typical problems we get called about:

  • You need development capacity for a project without adding permanent headcount
  • CVs look similar and your team loses hours interviewing unsuitable candidates
  • You are hiring for a skill nobody internally can assess properly
  • A recruitment partner is sending volume, but not filtered on technical ability
WHAT IT COVERS

What's included

  • Contract professionals for a defined period or project
  • Support identifying and evaluating candidates for permanent roles
  • Technical screening before candidates reach your interview stage
  • Collaboration with recruitment partners who already hold candidate pools
  • Flexible engagement rather than a fixed long-term commitment
§02
How it works

How the engagement works.

01

Client requirement

We work out what the role actually needs — the skills that matter, the ones that do not, and what the person will be doing day to day.

02

Recruitment partner

Where useful, we work alongside recruitment agencies who already have access to candidate pools. We are not trying to replace them.

03

Initial screening

Candidates are screened on technical and skill level by people who can evaluate the work, not just match keywords.

04

Shortlisting

Only candidates who make sense for the requirement move forward, with notes on where each one is strong and where they are not.

05

Client interview

You interview a shorter, better-filtered list. The final decision is always yours.

06

Hiring

Contract or permanent, depending on what suits the requirement.

Have something that needs doing?

Describe it in a line or two. You'll get a straight answer about whether we can help and what it would take.
