§01
What it is

What this actually is.

Most businesses need a handful of things built well — not a permanent engineering department. We work out what actually has to exist, build that, and leave you with something your own team can run.

WHO IT IS FOR

companies that need software built or improved

Typical problems we get called about:

  • You need software built but hiring a full development team is not justified yet
  • An existing application has stalled, or the person who built it has moved on
  • Off-the-shelf products almost fit your process, but not quite
  • You need a working version in front of customers before committing further
WHAT IT COVERS

What's included

  • Web applications and internal business software
  • Custom software built around how you already work
  • MVPs for startups that need something real in front of users
  • Maintenance and enhancement of software you already own
  • Integrations between the systems you already pay for
§02
How it works

How the engagement works.

Understand→Design→Build→Test→Launch→Support
01

Understand

We start with the business problem rather than a feature list — what has to change, for whom, and how you will know it worked.

02

Design

The smallest version that solves the problem properly. We say clearly what we are not building, and why.

03

Build

Working software from early on, reviewed with you regularly, so scope stays visible rather than being discovered at the end.

04

Test

Tested against the real cases your business runs into, not just the happy path.

05

Launch

Deployed to your infrastructure and your accounts, with documentation and a handover.

06

Support

A support arrangement agreed in writing, or a clean exit with everything documented. Both are fine.

Have something that needs doing?
