§01
The flow

Testing comes before
execution, not after it.

A strategy you have not tested is a guess. PORTVAX puts the testing first, and keeps the strategy yours — you can export it and take it with you.

Step 01

Idea

The rule you already trade by, or think you might. Written down instead of held in your head.

Step 02

Build

A visual drag-and-drop strategy builder. No coding required, and no black box handing you signals you cannot inspect.

Step 03

Backtest

Run it against more than ten years of historical data and see how the rule would actually have behaved.

Step 04

Paper trade

Run it in real-time market conditions with nothing at stake, until you believe it or you don't.

Step 05

Automate

Connect your own broker and let it execute, in an isolated environment with no shared infrastructure.

§03
Who it's for

Who it's for.

Retail traders, algo traders and developers. Traders working from unvalidated indicators and gut feel, or stuck on platforms that hand out signals instead of control. As PORTVAX puts it: trading without testing is just guessing.

PORTVAX is technology for building and testing strategies. It does not give financial advice, does not recommend strategies, and makes no claim about returns — what it offers is the ability to find out how your own rule would have behaved before you risk anything on it.
