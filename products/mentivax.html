§01
Overview

What Mentivax does.

Mentivax is built for schools still running on spreadsheets and paper. Fees, student records, attendance and parent communication live in one place instead of being spread across files, registers and someone's memory.

The part that makes it different is how you get information out of it. Rather than learning where every report lives, you ask in plain language and it answers.

§02
The problem

Who it's for.

For schools. Schools that are, in their own words, tired of Excel and paperwork. Administrative work expands to fill the week — chasing fees, updating registers, rebuilding the same reports, answering the same parent questions — and it comes out of time that should go to teaching.
