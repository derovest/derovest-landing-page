§01
The problem

School administration
arrives in pieces.

Mentivax is built for schools still running on spreadsheets and paper. Administrative work expands to fill the week — chasing fees, updating registers, rebuilding the same reports, answering the same parent questions — and it comes out of time that should go to teaching.

The information exists. It is just spread across files, registers, a WhatsApp group and somebody's memory, so nobody can answer a simple question without assembling it first.

Before

  • Fee registers in one file, student records in another
  • Attendance on paper, typed up later if at all
  • The same report rebuilt by hand every month
  • Parents phoning the office for things the office has to go and look up
  • One person who knows where everything is

With Mentivax

  • One record per student, fees and attendance attached to it
  • Attendance taken once, visible immediately
  • Reports that already exist, rather than being rebuilt
  • Parents told automatically over WhatsApp
  • Ask a question in plain language, get the answer
§02
Modules

One platform,
the modules you need.

Schools do not all run the same way. Mentivax is built as modules so a school can start with fees and attendance and add the rest when it wants to.

Students

One record per student, with everything attached to it rather than scattered across files.

Attendance

Taken once, visible straight away, and counted into the reports without anyone retyping it.

Fees & collections

What is due, what is paid, and who to remind — with invoices and receipts generated from the same record.

Staff & payroll

Staff records, leave, and payroll run from the same place as everything else.

Reports & dashboards

Accounts, expenses and the numbers a head teacher is asked for — already built, not rebuilt.

Parent communication

Fee reminders, attendance notices and announcements sent over WhatsApp, from the record itself.

Beyond the core

WhatsApp integration GPS vehicle tracking Transport management School website support Digital presence Smart paper correction

Schools ask for things that are not on anybody's feature list. Because Derovest builds Mentivax rather than reselling it, those requests go to the people who can actually add them.
