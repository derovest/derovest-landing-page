Why us

Why Derovest.

Practical technology

We build around the business problem in front of us, not around whatever is currently fashionable in engineering.

Lean development

We try to build the smallest thing that solves the problem properly, and we say clearly what we are leaving out.

Flexible talent

Contract professionals, hiring support, or both — sized to the requirement rather than to a minimum contract.

Automation first

Before building something new, we look at whether the repetitive work can simply stop being done by a person.

Product mindset

We build and own our own products, so we make the same trade-offs you do — not just the ones a supplier can bill for.

§05
How we work

Four steps, and no six-week
discovery phase.

STEP 01

Talk

A short conversation about what you actually need. If we are not the right people for it, we will say so.

STEP 02

Scope and price

Written scope, a price, and dates. Agreed before work starts so there are no discoveries later.

STEP 03

Build or staff

Software gets built in visible increments. Staffing requirements get screened and shortlisted.

STEP 04

Hand over

Code, documentation and accounts in your name. Support if you want it, a clean exit if you do not.
