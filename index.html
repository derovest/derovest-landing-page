What is Derovest?

Derovest is a technology company with two sides. It builds and owns its own software products — Mentivax, school management software, and PORTVAX, a no-code trading platform. It also provides technology services to other businesses: software development, technology staffing and business automation. Derovest works with software companies, startups and non-technology businesses, remotely, worldwide.

Those two sides are worth keeping separate, because most visitors only need one of them. Here is which is which.

§06
How we work

Four steps, and no six-week
discovery phase.

STEP 01

Talk

A short conversation about what you actually need. If we are not the right people for it, we will say so.

STEP 02

Scope and price

Written scope, a price, and dates. Agreed before work starts so there are no discoveries later.

STEP 03

Build or staff

Software gets built in visible increments. Staffing requirements get screened and shortlisted.

STEP 04

Hand over

Code, documentation and accounts in your name. Support if you want it, a clean exit if you do not.
