§01
The idea

Two things, done properly.

We build our own products. Mentivax — school management software — and PORTVAX, a no-code trading-strategy platform, are Derovest's own technology, built and run by us. Product work means we live with our own decisions rather than handing them over and moving on.

We help businesses use technology. Software development for companies that need things built. Technology staffing for companies that need people. Business automation for companies drowning in repetitive manual work.

Those two sides feed each other. What we learn running products makes the client work better, and the range of client problems keeps the products honest.

§02
How we think

How we approach the work.

Practical technology

We build around the business problem in front of us, not around whatever is currently fashionable in engineering.

Lean development

We try to build the smallest thing that solves the problem properly, and we say clearly what we are leaving out.

Flexible talent

Contract professionals, hiring support, or both — sized to the requirement rather than to a minimum contract.

Automation first

Before building something new, we look at whether the repetitive work can simply stop being done by a person.

Product mindset

We build and own our own products, so we make the same trade-offs you do — not just the ones a supplier can bill for.

§03
Who we help

Who we work with.

We are built to be practical and accessible — particularly for companies that cannot justify a large internal technology team, and should not have to.

Software & technology companies

You already build things. You need more capacity, or people you can trust to assess technical talent.

  • Additional developers on contract
  • Technology professionals for a defined project
  • Technical screening support when hiring
  • Extra software development capacity

Startups

You need something real in front of users, built by people who will tell you what not to build.

  • MVP development
  • Software engineering without a full team
  • Cost-conscious, practical builds
  • Technology talent as you grow

Non-technology businesses

You run on spreadsheets, email and manual steps. You do not want an IT department — you want the repetition gone.

  • Business and workflow automation
  • Internal software built around your process
  • Reporting that does not need rebuilding
  • Fewer repetitive administrative tasks
