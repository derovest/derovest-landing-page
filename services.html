HOW IT STARTS

How an engagement works

The same shape whichever service you need. Nothing starts until the scope and the price are written down and agreed.

  • Talk. A short conversation about what you actually need. If we are not the right people for it, we will say so.
  • Scope and price. Written scope, a price, and dates. Agreed before work starts so there are no discoveries later.
  • Build or staff. Software gets built in visible increments. Staffing requirements get screened and shortlisted.
  • Hand over. Code, documentation and accounts in your name. Support if you want it, a clean exit if you do not.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the problem rather than the solution. A short conversation is usually enough to work out whether it is a software job, a staffing job, or an automation job.
