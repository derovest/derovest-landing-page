What we do.

Service 01

Software Development

For companies that need software built or improved

Build what you need without building a large development team from scratch.

  • SaaS products, mobile applications, backend and APIs
  • MVPs for startups that need something real in front of users
  • Maintenance and enhancement of software you already own
  • Integrations between the systems you already pay for
Service 02

Business Automation

For businesses that want to reduce repetitive manual work

Read more

If your team repeats it every week, there's a good chance we can automate it.

  • Repetitive data entry between systems
  • Manual reporting and spreadsheet-based workflows
  • Repetitive administrative and back-office tasks
  • Approval and hand-off processes that rely on someone remembering
  • Connecting business tools that do not talk to each other
  • Custom workflow automation built around your process
Service 03

Technology Staffing

For companies that need technology professionals

Read more

Technology talent through contract and hiring models, screened by people who can read code.

  • Contract professionals for a defined period or project
  • Support identifying and evaluating candidates for permanent roles
  • Technical screening before candidates reach your interview stage
  • Collaboration with recruitment partners who already hold candidate pools
  • Flexible engagement rather than a fixed long-term commitment
Service 04

Cloud & Cost Optimisation

For teams paying more than they need to keep things running

Read more

Spend less keeping your software running, with changes staged so your service stays up.

  • Cloud and hosting bills read line by line, with what each line is for
  • Idle, oversized, duplicated and forgotten resources identified
  • Right-sizing based on measured usage, not on guesswork
  • Committed and reserved pricing applied where it genuinely pays back
  • Storage, backup and data transfer costs reviewed, not just compute
  • Every change staged with a rollback, so nothing goes dark
Service 05

SEO Optimisation

For businesses that want to be found in search

Read more

Get found for what you actually sell, and know which pages are doing the work.

  • Technical audit: indexing, crawling and what is blocking them
  • Titles, descriptions and heading structure that match real queries
  • Structured data so search engines understand what you are
  • Site structure and internal linking that spreads authority
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Search Console set up properly, with reporting you can read
§02
How we work

How an engagement works.

The same shape whichever service you need. Nothing starts until the scope and the price are written down and agreed.

Step 01

Understand

A short conversation about what you actually need. If we're not the right people for it, we'll say so.

Step 02

Scope

Written scope, a price, and dates — agreed before work starts, so there are no discoveries later.

Step 03

Build

Software is built in visible increments. Staffing requirements get screened and shortlisted.

Step 04

Improve

Code, documentation and accounts in your name. Support if you want it, a clean exit if you don't.

Direct communication, no account manager in between Written scope before work starts Transparent pricing Iterative delivery Clean handover Ongoing support available
§03
Engagement

Three ways to work with us.

Model 01

Project

Defined scope, defined delivery. You know what you're getting and what it costs before we begin.

  • Written scope, price and dates
  • Built in visible increments
  • Code and accounts in your name
Best when the requirement is already clear
Model 02

Monthly technology partner

Continuous development, maintenance and improvement — the technology side of the business, handled.

  • Ongoing development and enhancement
  • Maintenance of what you already own
  • Automation and infrastructure work as it comes up
Best when the work doesn't end at launch
Model 03

Dedicated capacity

Developer or team capacity sized to your requirement, working alongside your own people.

  • Contract professionals for a defined period
  • Technically screened before they reach you
  • Scaled to the requirement, not to a minimum
Best when you need hands, not a supplier
